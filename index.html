The floor

The machines are people

Every machine on the floor belongs to somebody. Ruby runs the Deluxe. The County Fair cabinet came in off a truck. They have opinions about how you play, and they are not shy about them.

You are not pulling a lever for money that does not exist. You are pulling it because it is how she talks to you.

The card

A casino that keeps a file on you

The loyalty card has ten tiers and the perks are literal — valet, a room rate, lounge access, a line you no longer wait in. It never tells you what it means. It does not have to. Everyone in the building treats you differently once you have one.

The deal

The house has already been paid

You bought the game. That is the entire transaction. There is no second currency, no energy timer, no ad break and nothing to buy again, ever.

Which is why we can do something no free-to-play slot can afford to: publish the real numbers.

Who made it

Made by someone who repairs these machines

There is a 1963 Bally Money Honey in my house. It was the first slot machine that could pay you out without calling an attendant over, and it is the reason casino floors look the way they do. There is another Bally that was my dad's. Four pinballs, two of which work. Two cocktail cabinets and a pair of jukeboxes.

I am a repairman through and through. I love breathing life into something retired or forgotten, that everyone else swore had outlived its purpose.

That is what this is. The cabinets are built as objects because that is what they are. If it feels like somebody cared about the hardware, that is why.

— the developer

How the art is made

Much of this game's 2D artwork is AI-assisted. Concepts and source images are generated and then edited, composited, keyed and assembled by hand into the finished assets you see.

Several things are not: all audio is produced with conventional tools — no generative audio models are used anywhere in this project. A significant amount of the art is drawn procedurally by code or modelled rather than prompted. There are human visual contributions from a contract designer.

No generative AI runs at runtime. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.
